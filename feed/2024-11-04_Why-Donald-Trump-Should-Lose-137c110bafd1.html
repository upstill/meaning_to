I sent this out in email, but in case you know any voters who are still undecided(!), ask them to read it. It’s as short as I could possibly make it.

Why Donald Trump Should Lose

It’s easy to understand how undecided voters could be confused, uncertain and/or cynical: when we’re engulfed by noise, it’s mind-boggling. So lets clear the air with the simplest, most clear-cut reasons why Donald Trump should be kept out of the White House.

  • George Washington is turning in his grave. It’s hard to grasp the danger to our democracy of someone for whom the Constitution is just so much toilet paper if it crosses his own political interests and his ego. Here we have a man who is just fine with an assault on Congress even if it meant hanging the Vice President, calling the assailants ‘heroes’, ‘hostages’, and ‘patriots’, intending to pardon them if elected — and still insisting that he won. From the Founding Fathers forward, patriotism has been defined by loyalty to the Constitution, especially the peaceful transfer of power and the sanctity of elections. By that standard, Donald Trump is the opposite of a patriot.
  • Disconnection from reality: Trump may be lying, or he may not actually know, that the US is not a crime-ridden hellhole, or that the American economy leads the world, or that California does not have an electricity crisis, or that climate change is real, or that there isn’t a bird cemetery under every windmill. Either way, the line between fact and fiction means nothing to him. But if you don’t have a leader who is in touch with reality, you might as well have a lunatic in charge. You wouldn’t hire a babysitter who is lost in their own head; why a President?
  • Agent of chaos: This country is losing its mind with disinformation and division. (Eating pets in Ohio?!? Death threats to election workers and hurricane recovery crews?!?) Call politicians unreliable, but Donald Trump is special: fomenting contempt and distrust among Americans is what he is about, the foundation of his appeal. Is this what leadership looks like? Is this who you are and what you hope for your country?
  • Everything else: The threat to the world order if Putin is allowed to annex Ukraine; the trail of fraud convictions (for his businesses, his charity, his University and his campaign), with worse in the future; his hapless first Presidency, when he couldn’t even build his wall with a compliant Congress, and let 130,000 people die unnecessarily of COVID-19; his obvious mental decline. What does it tell you that these things are just footnotes?

In the partisan, tribal swamp we live in today, consider how hard it is to get anyone to break with their party. But over half of Trump’s hand-picked cabinet (“the best people”), including his own vice president, have been driven to do just that, because Donald Trump is an unprecedented threat to who we are as a country. If these people are risking their own necks to save us from him, maybe we should listen.

By Steve Upstill on November 4, 2024.
