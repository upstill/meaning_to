Why Donald Trump Should Lose

One of the worst people ever to run for President of the USA stands a good chance of winning. I don’t know any undecided voters, but I assume they are dazzled because Trump has successfully “flooded the zone with shit” to where they can’t fit it all in their heads. So I suggest focusing on just enough to close the deal. So here is the minimalist case against Donald Trump. Please share with anyone who 

  • George Washington is turning in his grave. Starting with the Founding Fathers and for over 250 years, patriotism was defined by loyalty to the Constitution, especially the peaceful transfer of power and the sacredness of elections. And now we have a man who is just fine with an assault on Congress, calling the assailants ‘heroes’, ‘hostages’, and ‘patriots’, and intending to pardon them if elected — and still insisting that he won without a shred of evidence.
  • Reality Matters: Trump may be lying, or he may not actually know that violent crime has been dropping for 40 years, or that the American economy leads the world, or that California does not have an electricity crisis, or that there isn’t a bird cemetery under every windmill. Either way, we have to have a leader who is in touch with reality, otherwise you might as well have a lunatic at the helm. 
  • Time was when nations literally devoted half their budgets to national defense to avoid being conquered by their neighbors. Today that figure is a tenth of that. Why? Because the world has agreed that wars of conquest are out of bounds, and they back each other up on that. If Putin is allowed to annex Ukraine, that pact is shattered. If he gets away with it, then what?
  • Look at the man, and ask yourself if this is who you want representing America. The rest of the world is already somewhere between hilarity and horror that a country with the stature of the USA would choose such a dangerous buffoon — twice! And over half of the Trump cabinet (“the best”, in his words), including his own vice president, have broken with their party to object.

There’s much more to be said, of course. But what else do you need?
