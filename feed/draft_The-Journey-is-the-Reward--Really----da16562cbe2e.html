The Journey is the Reward? Really?!?

In any contest for Most Annoying Platitude, it’s hard to beat “The journey is the reward.” It reminds me of my star-crossed fishing career: decades of abject, humiliating failure to catch fish, always accompanied by the voice in my head chanting “The point of fishing isn’t to catch fish.” The only way to describe either of these thoughts is “consolation prize.” If you think that the experience of the journey is cold comfort for a failed goal, let me share with you what I’ve discovered about that.

David Whyte, “Close”
