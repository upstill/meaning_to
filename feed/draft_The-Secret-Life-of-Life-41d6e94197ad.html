The Secret Life of Life

I’ve always been fascinated by the gap between the surface of life — how it presents day-to-day, in public — and what lies beneath: the factors, processes and forces that grind away out of sight. And it seems I’m not alone: gossip, conspiracy theories, people love to dig down. And yet, the dive is like snorkeling in a life jacket: you can struggle all you want, but sooner rather than later you’re popped back to the surface against your will. Consequently, most of us live in the default world.
