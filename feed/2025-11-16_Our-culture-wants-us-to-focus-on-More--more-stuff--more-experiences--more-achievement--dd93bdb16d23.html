Our culture wants us to focus on More: more stuff, more experiences, more achievement. It takes work and attention to realize (notice!) that it's not about breadth, but depth. How much can you get out of this moment? The richness of our lives is directly related to how much we can open to this moment right here. Because there really is nothing else.

What makes me feel alive? Sitting over coffee with a dear friend and feeling the energy and connection blossom and feed on itself until I'm teary-eyed with gratitude for such a life and such community.
