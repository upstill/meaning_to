A Third of America is On Fire

It’s an enduring mystery: despite the plain fact that Donald Trump is the most hopeless President America has ever had, something like one-third of American adults cling to his leadership. Are they delusional? Do they like the idea of having such a vain, impulsive, grafting, mean, none-too-bright, vulgar, compulsively lying, self-absorbed leader? It’s finally come to me.

Look at it this way. The persistence of Donald Trump’s viability on the national stage is a laser-like spotlight on one thing: how toxic the Democratic brand has become across a wide swath of the population. Imagine: so many people put Trump on one side of the scale, and the appeal of the Democratic party on the other, and decide that it’s pretty much a wash.

Rather than focusing on the corrupt figure in the White House, we should be wondering how the hell Democrats figure so poorly as to not constitute an appealing alternative. It’s a staggering index of failure.

Is it because the Democratic Party has a disastrously poor record?

Is it because of corruption?

It’s true that the Biden administration failed to seriously address widespread concerns about immigration, either by taking steps to secure the border or speak up for the contributions immigrants past have made to the country, in the manner of Ronald Reagan and others. But the Biden administration didn’t start a historically stupid and hopeless war, they didn’t throw upwards of a hundred billion dollars sending armed thugs into the streets, and they didn’t enrich themselves and their children to the tune of billions of dollars. 

How can the scales be this off? I have a theory.
