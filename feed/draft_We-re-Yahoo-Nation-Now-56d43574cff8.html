We’re Yahoo Nation Now

Six months in, cringing before the onslaught that is the second Trump presidency, we’re already asking ourselves “How much of this can even be undone by saner heads?” Such tides have reversed in the past, but this is feeling like a more fundamental change, in ways that are hard to put your finger on. Here’s one: it’s not just that the Yahoos are in charge. We’re a Yahoo nation now.

What do I mean by a Yahoo?

The US has always had its fair share of Yahoos.

Beginning with WWII and particularly its aftermath, the United States acquired a certain stature in the world. Science. Inter-relatedness. Trade. Institutional norms. 

Let me first make an important distinction. I grew up with some (white) people that others would dismiss as ignorant or otherwise uncouth by uppity standards, education being one of their issues, or being less rigorous about social graces. Growing up with people like that taught me that grace, compassion, reflectiveness and insight are only loosely correlated with level of education.

Yahoo == impulsive, entitled, empowered thoughtlessness. Sloppy of thought, impulsive of action. The opposite of careful, modest and rigorous. Yahoo thinking would turn on a dime given the least bit of thought.

Yahoo thinking is blind to simple distinctions: between Trump being prosecuted for crimes, on the one hand, and wholesale attacks on the independence of law enforcement and casualizing investigation and prosecution of political enemies. Between foreign criminals hiding out here and undocumented workers who are otherwise an innocent, productive presence.

Never mind better or worse policy, simple stupidity is acceptable: turning our backs on the future of energy. shooting ourselves in the foot with energy policy.

In embarking down the path of immigration enforcement defined by the BBB, America is blithely walking into deep, dark state apparatus. In ten years that will be retrospectively obvious, but it’s only the most casual observer who can’t see it coming even now. We’re giving ICE a blank check to sweep people off the street and hold them indefinitely without any due process. Why is approving of this a Yahoo stance? Failing to make a distinction between booting the worst of the worst and just rounding up people at Home Depot

Dismissal of professional journalism. “Doing my own research”. Failing to distinguish between pros who make mistakes and slapdash clickbait.

Never mind the constitutionality of giving ICE a blank check and default approval of masked officers hauling people off the street: detention centers and no courts with no purpose than giving a fearsome impression of an America bristling with hostility to the outsider

Acting like science is just another elite occupation bleeding the population dry for no purpose other than their own aggrandizement.

Idiocy in the ascendant: anti-vax, creationist, flat-earth, chem-trails, Jan 6 revisionism

America First vs. cooperation, trust and free commerce.

Let’s be frank: this strain has always been present in America. But two things kept a lid on it for the most part: representative democracy and institutional norms.

None of this is news. What we’re discovering is that 1) those values were our inheritance from the founding, and 2) the only reason we hung onto them was that we had always hung onto them; 3) Trump has blown them away like a castle made of tissue paper.

We ain’t getting that back.
