Do the Obvious Thing!

It’s easy to get nervous about your performance if you’re preoccupied with edges, and it’s easy to be preoccupied with edges if you take seriously the advice that pours out of the Internet and performance-enhancing books. But what strikes me is how that urge to optimize distracts much-needed attention away from doing the obvious, from getting out of your own way.
