Actually, You ARE Living In A Simulation

Did The Matrix blow your mind? Even if you were familiar with the dorm-room idea that we’re living in a simulation, that movie really slammed it home. What if our whole life, the world we think we live in, is nothing but a carefully crafted, impenetrable illusion? How could we tell? What if there isn’t any solid ground under our feet, but only what we think is true?

The genius of The Matrix was to take an old thread of philosophy and mysticism (i.e., brains in a vat, the illusion of samsara) and transmogrify it into a propulsive, epic sci-fi plot, complete with gunplay, martial-arts spectacle and brain-melting special effects. But beneath the spectacle lurked an existential nightmare: embedded in a system that gives you no hint of its actual nature — or even its very existence: being fed a stream of artificial information that seems unshakably, undeniably real. You live out your days completely immersed in this construction, in serene ignorance of your actual circumstances. What more perfect bondage could there be?

But here’s the thing: this is the life we lead. Right here, right now. And it doesn’t take a red pill to expose it, just a little thought.

In short, the only difference between the Matrix and reality

If you doubt the ability of a brain-bound simulation to convince you of its reality, consider dreams. Within a dream, it generally doesn’t even occur to the dreamer that what they’re seeing isn’t 100% real — this in the face of physical inconsistencies, gaping holes and fantastical elements. Sometimes you can realize that you’re having a nightmare and pop out of it, but keep in mind that in dreaming, we have another reality to go back to.
