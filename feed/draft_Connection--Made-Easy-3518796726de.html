Connection, Made Easy

I don’t know if you’ve noticed, but the hive mind has been waking up to the problem/opportunity of simple human connection, the fact of life which brings us joy and makes us feel we are not alone in this world.

Here’s a thought worth thinking about: the need for connection is behind a whole symphony of modern quagmires; if not the cause, it’s a major contributing factor to so much of what ails us. And on the upside, properly serving that fundamental drive goes a long way to making life worth living. Pretty good thing to think about when you think about it.

The most salient, if not the most pervasive, is the so-called loneliness epidemic.

It’s been decades in the making

  • the “loneliness epidemic”
  • the decline of connective structures

I was recently visiting with an impending divorcé who has been diligently trying to develop a social network with, e.g., book groups, meetups and the like. 

So if making connection is such a problem, both for individuals and the society at largeBut I bring you good news. This is not a hard problem to solve
