## Beta App Description
RouzMe helps you finally get to the things you've been meaning to do. Collect
ideas — movies to watch, books to read, places to go, skills to learn — into
"Pursuits," and RouzMe serves one up at random when you're ready to act, so a
long list never feels overwhelming.

- Organize ideas into Pursuits, each with notes and links.
- Save anything from the web: on mobile via the Share sheet, on desktop via the
  "Send to RouzMe" browser extension — the app pulls in the title (and, for
  movies/TV, a synopsis) automatically.
- Get a random suggestion when it's time to do something. NOTE: RouzMe notices when you've passed on a suggestion, so you see it less in the future.
- Track what you've done, and share Pursuits with friends (read-only).

Sign in with email; your Pursuits sync across web, iOS, and Android.

## What to Test (per-build notes)
Try creating a Pursuit and adding a few Ideas, including by pasting a URL. On iOS, test sharing a link into RouzMe from Safari or another app (Share sheet → RouzMe). Pick a random suggestion ("Hit Me"), mark things done, and try sharing a Pursuit with someone via an invite link. Feedback on the capture flow and the random-suggestion experience is especially welcome.
